Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how our movie review platform collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the website.

Information We Collect

We may collect information such as your username, email address, and password when you create an account, reviews and ratings you post, and basic technical information such as browser type, device information, and general site usage.

How We Use Your Information

We use this information to create and manage user accounts, allow users to post reviews and ratings, improve the website and user experience, and monitor the platform for misuse or technical problems.

Public Content

Reviews and ratings you post may be visible to other users. Please do not share sensitive personal information in public posts.

Data Protection

We take reasonable steps to protect user information, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure. Users are responsible for keeping their account credentials private.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may only be shared when necessary for legal reasons, technical maintenance, or project administration.

Third-Party Services

This website may use third-party services or APIs to display movie-related data. These external services may have their own privacy practices, which are outside our control.

Data Retention

We keep user information only as long as needed for the operation and maintenance of the platform, unless removal is requested or required.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the project team through the available platform channels.
